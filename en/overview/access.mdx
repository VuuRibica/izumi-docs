---
title: "How access works"
description: "Who sees what in Izumi, and why."
---

Checked on Izumi 0.5.7, 2026-11-21.

Access in Izumi rests on three terms: company, domain and role. The same rules apply to you and to every AI tool you connect.

## Company, domain and role

- The company is the data boundary. Another company does not see your documents, sources or access records.
- Inside a company there are domains, areas of knowledge such as Finance or HR.
- A role binds a person to one domain. A member sees only documents from domains where they have a role.

| Role | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Reader** | Reads and searches the documents of a domain. |
| **Editor** | Everything a reader does, sends web clips into the domain and approves or rejects the proposed notes of that domain. |
| **Company admin** | Manages members, domains, sources and settings. Is not automatically an editor of any domain. |

## An AI tool has your rights, no more

- A connected AI tool sees only what you may see, in all your companies.
- When you connect over OAuth, reading is always on. Proposing notes is optional and you can switch it off when you connect. You can narrow a personal token to some companies and domains.
- The connection lasts at most 90 days. You revoke it yourself on **My tokens and profile**.

## How people get in

- By invitation only. The server operator creates the first one, a company admin creates all others. The link is valid for 72 hours and works once.
- Izumi does not send email (from the code). The admin sends the link through a channel they trust.
- There are no passwords: you sign in with a Google or Microsoft account. Both were checked live on Izumi 0.5.8: Google, and Microsoft with a work account on a test tenant.

## Who decides on proposals

- The editor of the domain the proposal is for decides, in the **Review queue**.
- A company admin who is not an editor does not see that domain's proposals. Every domain needs at least one editor, otherwise proposals wait until they expire.

## Who asked what

- The **Access log** records every call: from the web, from AI tools, from the web clip extension and through the push API. An admin sees everyone in the company, a member sees only their own.
- A document title in the log is visible only to someone who may read that document. Everyone else sees just a count.
- How long records are kept is set by the company, 365 days by default.

## Limits

- What you may not see appears in neither the results nor the result count.
